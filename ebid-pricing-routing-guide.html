6 · Salesforce eBids design noteFor dev reference
The routing guide above is process-driven and offline — it is not system-enforced in Salesforce. In the eBids build:
  • The Bid Coordinator (Sandra or Ginger) receives all bids and manually assigns approvers per this guide
  • The system does not auto-route based on dollar thresholds — the Coordinator makes that judgment call
  • Rebate programs are informational only — when a rebate is involved, the Coordinator may assign additional approvers per the guide, but no separate approval object is needed
  • The Coordinator can add, remove, or override approvers at any time per SFDC-25604 Scenarios 1–4
