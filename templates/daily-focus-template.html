👋 This is a preview — here's how to get your own personalized version

This file shows you what your daily focus doc will look like. To get your own version with your name, projects, and tasks — follow these steps:

  1. Go to claude.ai and start a new conversation
  2. Tell Claude about your work — paste or type something like:
    "I want to build a daily focus doc like the Newell Brands IT Toolbox template. My name is [name], my role is [role]. My current projects are [list them]. My top priorities this week are [list them]. I'm waiting on [person] for [item]."
  3. Claude builds your personalized file — download it and open it in your browser. It will have your name, your projects, and your tasks already filled in.
  4. Check items off as you go — progress saves to your browser automatically. No login needed.
  5. Update Claude throughout the day — just say things like "mark X as done", "add Y to today", "I'm now waiting on Z" and Claude updates your file
  6. Log your time as you go — fill in the time log at the bottom · use it to update your weekly time tracker on Friday

💡 Tip: The more context you give Claude upfront, the better your first version will be. You can always update it later!
