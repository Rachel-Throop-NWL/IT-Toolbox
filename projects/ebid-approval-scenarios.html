eBids — Approval Process · All Scenarios Updated Aug 6 2026

Process flows for all 4 approval scenarios · SFDC-25926 · One approval process · Bid reviewer controls approvers manually

Overview

There is one approval process in Salesforce. The bid reviewer manages approvers manually — there is no automated routing. The Bid Source field on the bid record determines what type of approval is being initiated and which of the four scenarios applies.

Bid reviewer retains full manual control of approvers in every scenario. The system routes and tracks — it does not assign approvers automatically.

BidVisible · requiredHiddenScenario A

Status flow

Created → Under Review → Submitted for Approval → Rejected or Approved

Approval type (Bid / Rebate Only / Bid + Rebate) is logged in the history at each submission.
