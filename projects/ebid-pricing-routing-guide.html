6 · Salesforce eBids design note

The routing guide above is process-driven and offline — it is not system-enforced in Salesforce. In the eBids build:

ItemDesign decision
Approver assignmentBid reviewer (coordinator) manually assigns approvers per this guide — system does not auto-route based on dollar thresholds
Bid SourcePicklist on bid record: Bid / Rebate Only / Bid + Rebate. Controls section visibility and determines approval scenario.
Approval historyBid Source value is captured in the approval history log at time of submission — provides audit trail of what was approved in each cycle
Rebate sectionHidden on the bid record until Bid Source = Rebate Only or Bid + Rebate — not a separate object
SAPNo SAP writes from eBids — all after-sales pricing only
