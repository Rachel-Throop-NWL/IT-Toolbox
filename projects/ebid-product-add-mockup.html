How to use this mockup
Step 1 — Toggle your role
Use the Viewing as dropdown in the top right of the line items table to switch between Sales rep and Coordinator / Director. The columns in the table and the fields in the product popup will change based on your role. Coordinator / Director shows additional restricted pricing fields marked with a lock icon.
Step 2 — Search for a product
Type any of the following in the search box above to find a product from the pricing guidelines:

rubbermaid coleman coffee oster ball calphalon

Or by material number: 100410071008
Step 3 — Review the popup
Click any result to open the confirmation popup showing pricing from the eBid Pricing Guidelines record. Coordinators and Directors will also see soft floor, hard floor, distributor current price, and public bid price. Click Add to bid to proceed.
Step 4 — Fill in line details and save
Fill in the editable fields: Line #, Units, IsCases, Requested price, Competitor, and Competitor price. Click Save line to commit to the bid. The pre-loaded line at the top shows an example of a completed line item.
