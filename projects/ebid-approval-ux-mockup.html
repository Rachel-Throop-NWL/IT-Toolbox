Daily reminder email behavior

If no action is taken, the approver receives a daily reminder: "eBid #99-001234 has been in your queue for X days. Click here to view the bid." Creates urgency and accountability. Single link — no VPN required (Salesforce is cloud-based).

Non-negotiables
Requirements that cannot be compromised
These must be confirmed with Venkat before build begins
1
Every approval email must include a direct link to the Salesforce bid record. Applies to all notification types — bid routed, daily reminder, final approved, denied. Approvers must be able to click directly into the bid from the email without searching for it.
2
All email notifications must support configurable additional recipients. The person submitting a change is not always the Requestor. Any notification type must allow additional recipients to be added. The option to turn off the email to the Requestor must also be available.
3
Completion logic must be enforced at the system level and cannot be bypassed via UI or automation. A bid cannot reach Final Approve status while any checked approver type has a pending Approval Request record.
4
Approved Approval Request records must be locked. Once an approver has approved, their assignment cannot be changed or removed. The system must prevent retroactive edits to approved records.
5
The Bid Coordinator must be able to add approvers mid-process. Adding a new approver after the approval process has begun must be supported. The bid cannot finalize until the newly added approver also responds.
